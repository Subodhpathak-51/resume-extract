## Design Context

### Users
Recruiting operations teams and administrators processing large resume batches. They need to upload, verify, correct, search, and export candidate data quickly while maintaining traceability.

### Brand Personality
Assured, precise, and operational. The product should feel calm under load, trustworthy with personal data, and fast without feeling disposable.

### Aesthetic Direction
A daylight operations console inspired by the supplied CareerSense reference: deep ink navigation, paper-white work surfaces, restrained teal success accents, and amber/red reserved for review states. Dense information remains legible and purposeful.

### Design Principles
- Make the next operational action obvious.
- Treat status colors as signals, not decoration.
- Preserve privacy with browser-local storage and explicit downloads.
- Prefer clear hierarchy and dividers over nested cards.
- Keep batch workflows useful across desktop and narrow screens.
